================================================================================

1. PROJECT NAME AND OVERVIEW

Official Name: UPSA Department of Information Technology Studies Platform (Web Portal & Central CMS Control Center)

Summary:
The UPSA Department of Information Technology Studies Platform is a complete web portal built for the Department of Information Technology Studies at the University of Professional Studies, Accra (UPSA). It serves as the primary digital gateway for the department, providing students, prospective applicants, and visitors with public access to academic degree programmes, course syllabi, faculty directories, student project showcases, event announcements, and a digital Learning Hub for course study materials. In addition, it includes a secure administrative portal (the Central CMS Control Center) that allows department leaders, faculty staff, and administrators to update website content, manage course materials, track visitor engagement, and delegate specific administrative permissions.

Ownership and Audience:
The platform is owned and operated by the Department of Information Technology Studies at UPSA. It serves three main audiences:
1. Students and General Visitors: Public users who browse academic offerings, search faculty profiles, view student project showcases, and download study resources.
2. Department Lecturers and Administrative Staff (Sub-Administrators): Authorized staff members who manage specific content areas such as course files, faculty profiles, or department announcements based on assigned duties.
3. Department Leadership (Super Administrators): Department heads and primary administrators who have full control over the platform, including creating user accounts, assigning staff permissions, resetting passwords, viewing audit logs, and monitoring visitor analytics.


================================================================================

2. PURPOSE AND GOALS

The Problem It Solves:
Before this platform, students and department staff faced several challenges:
- Academic information and study materials were scattered across different channels, making it difficult for students to find current course outlines, past exam questions, or lecture slides.
- Department announcements, student innovation projects, and faculty research achievements lacked a central online showcase.
- Administrative content updates required manual technical intervention, leading to outdated website information.
- Department leadership lacked visibility into user engagement, download statistics, and administrative actions.

Main Goals of the Platform:
1. Centralized Academic Resource Hub: Provide students with direct, instant online access to course outlines, lecture slides, past exam questions, handouts, and lab manuals filtered by academic year, semester, and level.
2. Digital Department Showcase: Highlight academic degree programmes, faculty qualifications, student software projects, and department events to promote the department to current students and prospective applicants.
3. Delegated Content Management: Allow department leadership to assign specific management rights to staff members (such as managing faculty profiles or course materials) without giving up full system control.
4. Transparency and Security: Maintain detailed audit logs of all administrative changes, enforce secure user logins, and gather anonymous visitor analytics to understand which resources are most useful to students.

3. USERS AND ROLES

The platform supports three primary user roles:

1. Public Visitors and Students (Unauthenticated Users)
- Who they are: Current UPSA students, prospective students, external visitors, and research partners.
- What they can see: Homepage slides, department overview, academic degree programmes, faculty directory, student showcase projects, developers hub details, event announcements, and the Learning Hub course catalog.
- What they can do: Search and filter courses by level (100 to 400) and semester, view detailed course information, download approved study resources (slides, past questions, handouts), search faculty members by research area, share deep links to specific courses, and submit inquiries to join the developers hub. They cannot edit any website content or access administrative tools.

2. Sub-Administrators (Authenticated Staff Members)
- Who they are: Lecturers, teaching assistants, or administrative staff granted access to specific management sections.
- What they can see: The public website plus assigned management sections in the Central CMS Control Center (such as Resource Management or Faculty Management).
- What they can do: Upload, edit, publish, unpublish, or delete content within their assigned modules. For example, a staff member with academic management permission can upload past questions and lecture slides for specific courses. They cannot create new administrator accounts or modify system settings.

3. Super Administrators (Authenticated Department Leadership)
- Who they are: Department heads, lead IT administrators, and designated system managers.
- What they can see: Everything on the platform, including all administrative management sections, system analytics, sub-admin user lists, and audit activity logs.
- What they can do: Complete control over all content modules, website navigation, academic programmes, curriculum settings, sub-administrator creation, permission assignments, account activation/deactivation, password resets, activity log reviews, and system configuration.

Simple "Who Can Do What" Summary:
- View public website and download study materials: Public Visitors, Students, Sub-Admins, Super Admins
- Upload and manage course study materials: Sub-Admins with Academic permission, Super Admins
- Edit homepage, faculty directory, and events: Sub-Admins with assigned permissions, Super Admins
- Create sub-admin accounts and assign permissions: Super Admins only
- Reset staff passwords and view audit activity logs: Super Admins only

5. SCOPE

In Scope (What the Platform Covers):
- Official public website for the UPSA Department of Information Technology Studies.
- Interactive course catalog and study resource download center (Learning Hub).
- Showcase of academic programmes, faculty profiles, and student software projects.
- Event announcement modal and registration inquiry system for the student developers hub.
- Complete administrative control center for content management, staff permission delegation, visitor analytics, and audit logging.
- Deep-linking and URL bookmarking for specific academic courses and levels.

Out of Scope (What the Platform Does Not Do):
- Online assignment submission or grading for students (this is handled by UPSA's main university learning management system).
- Official student grade records, fee payments, or official university registration.
- Live chat, real-time messaging, or video conferencing between students and lecturers.
- Direct automated email sending from inside the web browser (email notifications rely on external service triggers or user email clients).

Assumptions and Limitations:
- It is assumed that students have standard web browsers on desktop or mobile devices to access the portal.
- Uploaded study files are limited to a maximum size of 50 megabytes per file to maintain optimal storage performance.
- Administrator account creation requires an active Super Administrator to perform the initial setup.

6. SYSTEM ANALYSIS

Main Parts of the Platform:
The platform consists of three main parts working together:
1. The Web Frontend: The interactive pages and administrative screens that users see in their web browser.
2. The Database Service: A cloud database that securely stores structured information such as course details, resource links, staff profiles, sub-admin permissions, and activity logs.
3. The Serverless Cloud Functions: Independent background services that perform administrative actions requiring elevated security, such as creating new sub-admin accounts or updating passwords safely.

Main User Journeys:

Journey 1: A Student Finding and Downloading a Lecture Slide
1. The student opens the website and clicks on "Learning Hub".
2. The student selects "Level 200" and "Semester 1".
3. The platform displays matching courses, such as "BITM 204: Database Management Systems".
4. The student clicks on the course card to open the Course Detail view.
5. The student reviews available materials under "Lecture Slides" and clicks "Download".
6. The resource file opens or downloads directly, and the system records an anonymous resource access event for analytics.

Journey 2: An Administrator Uploading a Past Exam Question
1. The lecturer logs into the Central CMS Control Center.
2. The lecturer selects "Resource Management" from the admin sidebar menu.
3. The lecturer clicks "Add New Resource".
4. The lecturer selects the target course, enters the resource title (e.g. "2025 Mid-Semester Exam"), selects resource type ("Past Question"), and selects the PDF file from their computer.
5. The system checks the file format and size, uploads the file securely, and creates the course resource entry.
6. The past question immediately becomes available for students in the Learning Hub.

Journey 3: A Super Administrator Creating a Sub-Admin Account
1. The Department Head logs into the admin portal as a Super Administrator.
2. The Head selects "Admin Management" and clicks "Provision New Sub-Admin".
3. The Head enters the new staff member's full name, official email, and initial password (using the show-password toggle to check for typos).
4. The Head selects specific authorized areas (such as "Manage Programmes & Curriculum" and "Manage Faculty Directory").
5. The system securely creates the staff account, assigns selected permissions, and records the event in the Activity Audit Log.

Information Stored by the Platform:
- Administrators and Profiles: Full name, official email, assigned role (Super Admin vs Sub Admin), active status, last login date, and granted module permissions.
- Courses and Curriculum: Course code, course title, academic level, semester, course classification (Core, Elective, Required), course description, credit hours, and course outline web link.
- Learning Resources: Resource title, description, resource classification (past question, slide, handout, lab manual, tutorial guide, video link), file web link, internal storage path, academic year, publication status, and display order.
- Faculty Directory: Staff member name, academic designation, email address, phone number, office location, research interests, and profile photo link.
- Student Projects: Project title, category, description, student developer name, project mentor name, technologies used, GitHub repository link, and live demo link.
- Activity Logs: Historical record of administrative actions, showing who performed the action, what was changed, and when it occurred.
- Visitor Analytics: Anonymous records of page visits and resource opens used to measure popular study materials.

External Services Dependent On:
- Web Hosting Service (Vercel): Hosts the website files so the portal is available online 24/7.
- Cloud Database and Authentication Service (Supabase): Handles user logins, password encryption, database storage, and file hosting.

9. SYSTEM DESIGN (PLAIN LANGUAGE)

Overall Design Approach:
The platform is designed as a modern, unified web application that contains both the public website and the private administrative portal within a single platform. The public side focuses on high speed, clarity, and ease of use for students browsing on mobile phones or computers. The administrative side is designed like a professional dashboard with a dark theme, clear navigation sidebar, status badges, data tables, and confirmation popups to prevent accidental data loss.

Page and Navigation Structure:
The platform uses web address tags (hash routes) to move between pages smoothly without needing to reload the entire screen:
- Main Landing Page (#/): Shows the homepage hero banner, department overview, degree programmes, faculty summary, student projects, developers hub, and footer.
- Faculty Directory Page (#/faculty): Dedicated searchable page for faculty members.
- Learning Hub Page (#/learning-hub): Dedicated academic study library with course filters and detailed modal views.
- Admin Portal Gateway (#/admin): Protected login screen and administrative dashboard.
- Password Reset Page (#/reset-password): Secure password recovery page.

How the Admin Side and Public Side Relate:
The public side and admin side are directly connected through the shared cloud database. When an administrator adds a new course outline, updates a faculty member's office location, or publishes a new lecture slide in the admin portal, that change becomes visible to students on the public website immediately.

10. GAPS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Features the Platform Lacks (Identified Gaps):
1. Automated Email Notifications: The system does not currently send automated email alerts to students when new course materials are published.
2. Direct Student Login Accounts: Currently, only administrators have accounts; students access materials as public users without individual student profiles.
3. Automated Backup Export Tool: While the cloud database maintains automatic backups, there is no one-click manual export button in the admin interface for downloading local database backups.

Suggested Next Features (in priority order):
1. Student Email Alerts for New Uploads
   Reason: Notifies students automatically whenever a lecturer uploads new past questions or lecture slides for their registered courses.
2. Filter by Academic Year in Learning Hub
   Reason: Helps students quickly separate current year study materials from older historical archives.
3. One-Click Admin Backup Export
   Reason: Provides department heads with an easy way to download a complete backup copy of all course records and audit logs to local office storage.
